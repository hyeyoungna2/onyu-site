Support

If you have a question or something isn't working, tell us in the app (MY → Contact us) or by email. We reply to the email address you write from.

Email: onyu.help@gmail.com

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to MY → Delete account. Deleting your account erases your account and records and cannot be undone. Pets you keep records for together with family stay with the remaining family members. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address below and tell us which account you signed up with.

How do I cancel ONYU Plus?

Go to iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. After you cancel, you can keep using ONYU Plus until the end of the period you have paid for.

When does a reply to my letter arrive?

After you send a letter, your pet's reply arrives the next morning. You can read it in Rainbow → Mailbox.

If I put away a memorial stone, are its records lost?

No. Letters, diary entries and guestbook entries all stay. You can bring the stone back at any time.

What do I do when my pet crosses the rainbow bridge?

In MY → My Pets, tap your pet and choose “Cross the rainbow bridge” at the bottom of the pet’s details, then enter the date. All records stay, and you can place a memorial stone in the Rainbow Garden.

What if I see an inappropriate post or member?

Use the ··· menu on a guestbook entry to report it or to block the member. Reported entries are hidden right away and reviewed by us. You can undo a block in MY → Privacy & visibility → Blocked users.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

후아영 (the "Operator") operates ONYU (the "Service") and sets out this Privacy Policy to protect the personal information of the people who use the Service and to handle related concerns promptly, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Personal information we process

  • Sign-up and sign-in (required): your email address and password (stored encrypted), or the account identifier and email address from Kakao, Apple or Google; your nickname
  • Browsing before sign-up: a temporary account identifier
  • Pet information (name required, the rest optional): name, kind, breed, sex, birthday, the day you started living together, the day your pet crossed the rainbow bridge, photos
  • Records (optional): diary entries, letters, health records (weight, vaccinations, vet visits, medication), schedules, growth timeline, photos and videos in Memory Box
  • Personalization (optional): your character, your memorial stone's design and words, profile photo
  • Content other members can see (optional): guestbook entries, replies, and photos, posts and comments on the memory board
  • Family invitations (optional): the account identifier of the person you invite and their relationship to your pet (for example, mom or dad)
  • ONYU Plus subscription (optional): the subscription product, its period and status, and the purchase confirmation number issued by Apple (payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple; the Operator never receives them)
  • Reports and blocks (optional): what you reported and why, and the members you blocked
  • Support requests (optional): what you wrote, any screenshots you attached, and the email address for our reply
  • Created automatically while you use the Service: access times, IP address, sign-in records

2. Why we process it

  • To identify members, let you sign in, manage accounts and prevent misuse
  • To provide the Service: pet records, the memorial space, films and other features
  • To let you keep records together with your family
  • To send service emails such as sign-up verification codes
  • To confirm and manage ONYU Plus subscriptions
  • To answer your questions and share notices
  • To keep the Service stable and fix errors

3. How long we keep it

  • When you delete your account, we delete your information without delay. Pets and records you keep together with family stay with the remaining family members; if no family remains, they are deleted too.
  • Guestbook entries and replies you left in other members' spaces are deleted with your account.
  • Information that Korean law requires us to keep is kept for that period:
    • Records of contracts, withdrawal of offers, payments and supply of goods: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
    • Records of consumer complaints and dispute handling: 3 years (same Act)
    • Service access records: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

4. Sharing with third parties

We process personal information only for the purposes in Section 2 and do not provide it to third parties unless you agree or the law specifically requires it. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. Service providers and transfers outside Korea

We entrust the following work to service providers:

  • Supabase Inc.: member authentication, storage and management of data and photos
  • Plus Five Five, Inc. (Resend): sending service emails such as sign-up verification codes

Because these providers are companies outside Korea, personal information is transferred abroad as follows:

  • Supabase Inc. (United States, privacy@supabase.com)
    • Information transferred: the items in Section 1
    • Purpose: member authentication and data storage. Data is stored on servers in the Republic of Korea (Seoul region) and may be accessed from abroad for operation and maintenance.
  • Plus Five Five, Inc. (United States, support@resend.com)
    • Information transferred: email address and the content of service emails
    • Purpose: sending service emails
  • When and how: sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) while you use the Service
  • Retention: until you delete your account or the service contract ends
  • If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can stop its processing by deleting your account. In that case you will not be able to use the Service.

Kakao, Apple and Google sign-in is used only to confirm your identity with the account you choose, and the information we receive from them is limited to the sign-up and sign-in items in Section 1.

6. What other members can see

  • The memorial stone and words you place in the Rainbow Garden, flowers and candles, guestbook entries, and the photos, posts and comments you put on the memory board are visible to other members, together with your nickname.
  • Diary entries, letters, health records, schedules and Memory Box photos are visible only to you and the family you invite.

7. Your rights and how to use them

  • You may at any time ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it.
  • In the app: edit your profile and pet information, delete records, export records (MY → Export records), delete your account (MY → Delete account)
  • For anything else, use MY → Contact us or the contact in Section 12. We will act without delay.
  • You may also exercise these rights through a legal representative or someone you authorize.

8. How we delete information

  • When the retention period ends or the purpose has been fulfilled, we delete the information without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.
  • Information we must keep by law is stored separately from other information and deleted when the period ends.

9. How we keep it safe

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted password storage
  • Access control: row-level security so that each member can reach only their own private information; server-only secret keys are never put in the app
  • Keeping the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum, and keeping access logs

10. Cookies and similar tools

The Service does not use cookies or similar tools that collect your information automatically.

11. Children under 14

The Service does not accept sign-ups from children under 14. If we learn that a member is under 14, we delete the account and its information without delay.

12. Privacy officer

The Operator has a privacy contact who oversees the processing of personal information, handles related complaints and remedies, and receives requests to access information.

  • Contact: 후아영, Privacy
  • Email: onyu.help@gmail.com

13. Where to get help

For advice on or to report a privacy violation, you may contact the following Korean agencies:

  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: +82 1833-6972 (www.kopico.go.kr)
  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center: 118 (privacy.kisa.or.kr)
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office: 1301 (www.spo.go.kr)
  • Korean National Police Agency: 182 (ecrm.police.go.kr)

If you live outside Korea, you may also contact the data protection authority where you live.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy applies from November 11, 2026. If it changes, we will announce it in Notices at least 7 days before it takes effect, and at least 30 days before for changes that significantly affect your rights.
